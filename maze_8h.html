◆ Cell

typedef uint8_t Cell

Represents a single cell in the maze.

Every cell is an 8-bit value containing both wall and exploration information.

The four least significant bits represent whether a wall exists in each direction (1=true, 0=false), while the four most significant bits represent whether that direction has been explored (1=true, 0=false).

The bit layout (from most significant bit to least significant bit):

+-------+-------+-------+-------+-------+-------+-------+-------+
| 7 | 6 | 5 | 4 | 3 | 2 | 1 | 0 |
+-------+-------+-------+-------+-------+-------+-------+-------+
| North | East | South | West | North | East | South | West |
+-------+-------+-------+-------+-------+-------+-------+-------+
| Explored | Walls |
+-------------------------------+-------------------------------+

◆ Maze

typedef Cell** Maze

Represents a maze as a 2D array of cells.

See Cell for details on the Cell data structure.

A maze has the following properties:

  • It is always square, with a side length of size.
  • The goal is always located at the center of the maze, at coordinates (size/2, size/2).
    • If the maze has an even size, the goal occupies a 2x2 area in the center.
  • The maze will always start at (0, 0).

Accessing a specfic cell at (y, x) can be done using maze[y][x]. In the north direction, the y coordinate decreases while in the south direction, the y coordinate increases. In the east direction, the x coordinate increases while in the west direction, the x coordinate decreases.
